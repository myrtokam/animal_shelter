About us

A calmer way to care for animals.

OKAPI is a platform for organisations that look after animals — from a small rescue to a livestock unit, a zoo or a wildlife organisation. We start with health and welfare, and grow into everything else a team needs to run well.

Vision & mission

Two goals, one platform

Everything we build has to serve at least one of these.

1

A better life for the animals

Whether an animal is a pet, a farm animal, a zoo resident or a rescue waiting for a home, its health, food, exercise and living conditions should be recorded, followed and improved over time — and the story should follow the animal wherever it goes.

2

Better organisation for the people

Shelters, associations, breeders and clinics run on limited time and money. We want to give them clear tools for administration, planning and day-to-day decisions, so more of their energy goes to the animals and the cause. That covers premises, equipment, staff, volunteers, supplies, accounts, marketing and events, as well as the animals themselves.

Our story

Why we are building this

OKAPI began as a software project and became something we believe in: a practical product for real organisations.

The idea is simple. Organisations that care for animals often keep their records in notebooks, spreadsheets and chat groups. Information gets lost when people change, and it is hard to see the bigger picture — what is working, what costs the most, what is likely to need attention next.

We want to replace that scattered picture with one clear workspace, starting with the part that matters most: the health of the animal.

Care is personal

Every animal, not only the usual ones

A dog at a shelter, a goat on a farm, a duck in a garden, an antelope in a reserve: each one has needs, a history and people who care about it.

That is why OKAPI is designed for many kinds of animals and organisations, not a single kind of clinic. The record stays simple; the care it supports can be as varied as the animals themselves.

The name

Why an okapi?

The okapi is a shy forest animal from Central Africa, often called the “forest giraffe”. It is rarely seen, easy to overlook and in need of careful protection.

It is a good reminder of what this work is about: paying attention to animals that are easy to miss. Our okapi wears a vet’s coat because care starts with looking closely. The name is pronounced oh-KAH-pee.

What guides us

Simple to use
